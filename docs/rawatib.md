# Shalat Rawatib (Sunnah Prayers)

Rawatib prayers are voluntary (sunnah) prayers performed immediately before (Qabliyah) or after (Ba'diyah) the five daily obligatory (Fardhu) prayers. They are highly encouraged to complement any deficiencies in the obligatory prayers.

Here are the rules, types, and timings for Rawatib prayers based on Islamic teachings:

## 1. Types of Rawatib Prayers

Rawatib is divided into two types based on how often the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) performed them:

### Rawatib Muakkad (Highly Encouraged)

These are 10 or 12 rakaats that the Prophet (PBUH) almost never missed.

- 2 rakaats before Subuh (Very highly emphasized).
- 2 or 4 rakaats before Zuhur.
- 2 rakaats after Zuhur.
- 2 rakaats after Maghrib.
- 2 rakaats after Isya.

### Rawatib Ghairu Muakkad (Less Emphasized)

These are additional prayers that the Prophet (PBUH) sometimes performed and sometimes omitted.

- 2 or 4 rakaats before Asr.
- 2 rakaats before Maghrib.
- 2 rakaats before Isya.

## 2. The 12-Rakaat Rule (Sunnah Muakkad Goal)

A popular, highly encouraged routine is to complete 12 rakaats of Sunnah Muakkad daily. The Prophet (PBUH) said:

> "Whoever prays 12 rakaats in a day and night, a house will be built for him in Paradise" (HR. Muslim).

- 4 Rakaats before Zuhur
- 2 Rakaats after Zuhur
- 2 Rakaats after Maghrib
- 2 Rakaats after Isya
- 2 Rakaats before Subuh

## 3. Execution Rules (Tata Cara)

- **Niat (Intention):** Expressed in the heart, explicitly mentioning the prayer as Qabliyah or Ba'diyah (e.g., "I intend to pray 2 rakaats qabliyah of Subuh... for Allah Ta'ala").
- **Rakaat Structure:** Generally, Rawatib prayers are performed in units of two rakaats per taslim (salam).
- **Location:** Ideally, these should be performed in a different spot from where the Fardhu prayer was just held (e.g., move slightly to the side).
- **Qadha (Makeup):** If a person regularly performs Rawatib but misses it, it can be made up (qadha) after the Fardhu prayer.
- **Speed:** Qabliyah Subuh should be performed quickly (reciting short surahs).

## 4. Times of Execution

- **Qabliyah:** Performed after the Adhan (call to prayer) and before the Iqamah.
- **Ba'diyah:** Performed immediately after the Fardhu prayer is completed.
