# Logistics & Baggage Rules

1. Liquids/Aerosols/Gels (LAGs): >100ml must go in check-in. Cabin: max 10 bottles (each max 100ml)

2. Power Bank: NEVER in check-in luggage. Must be in cabin bag. Max 20,000 mAh.

3. Check-in Baggage: Saudi Airlines = 23 kg × 2. Must use Patuna official luggage.

4. Cabin Bag: Max 7 kg per jemaah.

5. Laundry: Available in Makkah & Madinah via Mutawwif (personal cost).
